## Overview

**Calculator** is an all-in-one calculator for everyday maths and money planning: a basic and engineering calculator alongside EMI, loan, interest, GST, discount and tip calculators.

## What it does

- **Calculator:** the four basic operations plus engineering functions, on a large, easy-to-read keypad.
- **Loans:** work out monthly EMIs for personal, business and home loans.
- **Shopping and tax:** see savings, tax and the final total with the discount and GST calculators.
- **Tips:** calculate a tip and split the bill evenly.
- **After-call screen:** optionally open the calculator right after a call to total or split a bill.
