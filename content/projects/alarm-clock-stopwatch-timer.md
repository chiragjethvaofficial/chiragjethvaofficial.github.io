## Overview

**Alarm Clock - Stopwatch & Timer** brings an alarm clock, world clock, stopwatch and countdown timer together in one app with a clean, modern interface.

## What it does

- **Alarm:** set multiple one-time or recurring alarms with custom tones, snooze and repeat settings.
- **World clock:** keep track of the time in cities around the world.
- **Stopwatch:** time workouts or tasks, with laps.
- **Timer:** run several countdowns at once, with optional auto-restart.
