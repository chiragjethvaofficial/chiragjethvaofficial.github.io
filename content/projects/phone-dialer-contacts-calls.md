## Overview

**Phone Dialer: Contacts & Calls** is a lightweight default phone app for faster calling, cleaner contact management and fewer unwanted calls.

## What it does

- **Call faster:** a big dialpad with T9 search, speed dial, one-tap redial and a clean full-screen call screen.
- **Block unwanted calls:** add spam, telemarketing or unknown numbers to a block list in one tap.
- **Contacts:** add, edit and organise contacts, merge duplicates and keep a favourites list.
- **Call history:** missed, incoming and outgoing calls in separate tabs, with a summary after every call.
- **Comfort:** dark mode, adjustable text size and offline calling.

Contacts and call history stay on the phone; the Contacts, Phone and Call Log permissions are used only to run the dialer.
