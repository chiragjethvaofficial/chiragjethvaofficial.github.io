## Overview

A gallery with a private vault: hide photos and videos behind a pattern lock or fingerprint / face unlock, plus a photo editor and recycle bin.

## How it works

AndroidX Biometric secures the vault, WorkManager loads media in the background, and Play Billing unlocks premium features. Images are handled by Glide and Coil with support for animated WebP, APNG and SVG.
