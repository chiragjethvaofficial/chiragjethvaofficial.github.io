## Overview

**Contacts** keeps your phone contacts organised and makes calling quick, with a clean dialer, call history and fast search.

## What it does

- **Contact manager:** add, update or remove contacts, and find them instantly by name or number.
- **Dialer:** place calls from a smooth dialpad and redial from recent calls with a single tap.
- **Call history:** keep recent calls organised and easy to review.
- **After-call screen:** see call details after each call, then call back or save the number.

To provide calling features, the app can be set as the default phone app. Contacts and call data stay on the device.
