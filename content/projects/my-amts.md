## Overview

**My AMTS** is the official city bus app for the Ahmedabad Municipal Transport Service (AMTS) in Gujarat. It helps citizens find bus routes and nearby stops and follow their bus live, making daily commuting easier.

## My role

I developed and maintained the app at Cursor Soft as part of its government and public-transport work, contributing to live bus tracking and the route and stop features.

## What it does

- **Live bus tracking:** see where a bus is on the map, with its number and route.
- **Nearby stops:** find the AMTS bus stops closest to you.
- **Route navigation:** explore city bus routes and pick the best one for a journey.
- **Timetables and stations:** look up timetables, stations and stops across Ahmedabad.
